# Changelog

## 0.1.0, unreleased

The prototype, in one piece:

- The tiered store (native, read, derived, agent, expert) over public Saskatchewan data, with a readiness gate.
- A 2 km grid over the Athabasca Basin, 25 features, three scores and the effort null under spatial folds.
- The reading pipeline and the extractor agent with a second model family and a review queue.
- The analyst: a single-call baseline and the staged loop with a node gate, a verifier and two deciders.
- The interface agent with fixed question kinds, an abstain tool, insight recording and analyst jobs.
- The fabrication gate at every boundary, with its own measured suite.
- The MCP server, the API with roles and background jobs, the dashboard with a ten-step tour.
- The seed pack for a fresh clone, over S3 or a static host.
- UraniumBench: the frozen analyst benchmark, with the single-call arms, the cheap staged arm and the strong-stack
  staged arm scored on every open cell and the ablation matrix still incomplete; the interface tiers, built
  and not yet run.
